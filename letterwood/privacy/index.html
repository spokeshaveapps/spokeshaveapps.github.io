Letterwood privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026 · Letterwood for Android (package com.spokeshaveapps.letterwood) · by Spokeshave Apps

In short: Letterwood is an alphabet and handwriting app for young children. It collects no personal information and no data about how your child learns. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no servers. Everything it remembers (children's names and pictures, progress, stickers, settings) stays on your device. If you buy Pro, Google Play handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

1. Who this app is for

Letterwood is designed for children aged 2 to 7 and the grown-ups who look after them. It follows Google Play's Families policy. It does not include any advertising, analytics, social or sign-in services.

2. What the app stores, and where

WhatWhereWhy
Child profiles: an optional first name, a picture chosen from the app's own set, an age band, an alphabet and a left-handed settingOn your device onlySo each child keeps their own alphabet and progress, and (with Pro) can trace their name
Progress: letters traced and written, stars, and letters that are often mixed up (such as b and d)On your device onlyFor the progress page in the grown-ups area and to suggest letters to practise
Stickers earnedOn your device onlyFor the sticker book and the path
Settings: voices, volumes, play timer (the time played counts only while the app is open and is not saved), guide lines, music, haptics, reduced motion, languageOn your device onlySo the app remembers what a grown-up chose
Whether Pro has been boughtOn your device, and in your Google Play accountSo Pro keeps working without internet

What a child draws is checked on the device while they trace and is not kept as a picture. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it. A grown-up can also remove a child, or reset a child's progress, in the grown-ups area.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy
Google Play billingTo buy Letterwood Pro and restore it on a new device.
Internet, network stateAdded by Google Play's billing library so that Google Play can complete or restore a purchase. The app itself sends nothing over the internet, and every letter, voice and game works offline.

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or the advertising ID. Every voice in the app is built in; nothing your child says is heard or recorded.

4. Purchases

Letterwood Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play, kept behind a grown-ups check. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. We receive no payment or card details. A purchase is confirmed by Google Play to the app on your device.

5. Contacting us

The grown-ups area has a feedback button that opens your own email app, addressed to us, with the app version and phone model filled in. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email, and you can see everything in it first. We use what you send only to reply and to improve the app, and delete it on request.

6. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (US COPPA), children under the age of digital consent in the EU and UK (GDPR), and children under 18 (India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act). A child's name, if a grown-up enters one, never leaves the device. If you believe the app has collected information about your child, write to us and we will help.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
