Hum & Drum privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Hum & Drum for Android (package com.spokeshaveapps.humanddrum) · by Spokeshave Apps

In short: Hum & Drum is a toy instrument app for young children. It collects no personal information and no data about how your child plays. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no servers. Everything it remembers (settings, stickers, recordings) stays on your device. If you buy Pro, Google Play handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

1. Who this app is for

Hum & Drum is designed for children aged 2 to 8 and the grown-ups who look after them. It follows Google Play's Families policy. It does not include any advertising, analytics, social or sign-in services.

2. What the app stores, and where

WhatWhereWhy
Settings: volume limit, play timer, key labels, language, sound choicesOn your device onlySo the app remembers what a grown-up chose
Stickers earned and minutes played todayOn your device onlyFor the sticker book and the grown-ups area
Recordings made in the appOn your device onlyA recording is a list of the notes your child tapped, played back by the app. The app never uses the microphone or camera.
Whether Pro has been boughtOn your device, and in your Google Play accountSo Pro keeps working without internet

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy
Google Play billingTo buy Hum & Drum Pro and restore it on a new device.
Internet, network stateAdded by Google Play's billing library so that Google Play can complete or restore a purchase. The app itself sends nothing over the internet, and every instrument works offline.

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or the advertising ID.

4. Purchases

Hum & Drum Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play, kept behind a grown-ups check. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. We receive no payment or card details. A purchase is confirmed by Google Play to the app on your device.

5. Contacting us

The grown-ups area has a feedback button that opens your own email app, addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email, and you can see everything in it first. We use what you send only to reply and to improve the app, and delete it on request.

6. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (US COPPA), children under the age of digital consent in the EU and UK (GDPR), and children under 18 (India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act). If you believe the app has collected information about your child, write to us and we will help.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
