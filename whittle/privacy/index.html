Whittle privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Whittle for Android (package app.whittle) · by Spokeshave Apps

In short: Whittle compresses your photos on your phone. Your photos, file names and locations are never uploaded to us or anyone else. We have no servers and no accounts. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data under the ad privacy choices you make. Buying Pro removes ads.

1. Your photos

Whittle reads only the photos you choose in the Android photo picker, share to Whittle, or (if you turn on the Pro “Auto-compress screenshots” feature) the one folder you grant through the Android folder picker. All decoding, resizing, cropping and compression happens on your device. Compressed copies are saved to your phone’s Pictures folder (PDFs to Download). Originals are never changed; if you ask Whittle to move an original to the bin, Android asks you to confirm first.

By default Whittle removes location (GPS) and other metadata from the copies it makes. You can change this per photo.

2. Permissions

PermissionWhy

Whittle does not request storage, media, camera, location, contacts or microphone permissions.

3. What stays on your phone

Whittle keeps these in its private app storage, and only on your device: your settings, the “Recent” list (names and sizes of compressed copies, so you can reopen them), saved presets, the folder you chose for auto-compressed screenshots. Your Pro status is not stored; it is asked from Google Play each time. We cannot see any of it. The Recent list is excluded from Android backups. Your other settings may be included in your own Android device backup to your Google account, under your control and Google’s terms; we have no access to that backup.

4. Ads (free version only)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Before any ad request, Whittle uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in some US states). You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

When ads are shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google: IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad and product interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID. Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Whittle Pro users see no ads. Once Google Play confirms your Pro purchase, the ads SDK and consent form are not started at all.

5. Purchases

Whittle Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app receives only whether you own Pro. See the Google Payments privacy notice.

6. Bug reports and feature requests

Settings → Report a bug / Request a feature prepares an email in your own email app. Nothing is sent until you tap Send there. It contains only what you type and, if you leave the switch on, the app version, Android version, phone model and app language. It never contains photos, file names or locations. We use these emails only to fix bugs and plan features, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

7. Analytics

Whittle has a “usage statistics” setting that is off by default. This version of Whittle contains no analytics service, so no usage data is sent either way. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first and the data will never include photos, file names or locations.

8. Children

Whittle is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them.

9. Deleting your data

Everything Whittle stores is on your phone: clear it with Android Settings → Apps → Whittle → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Compressed copies are normal photos in your gallery that you can delete any time. To have a feedback email you sent us deleted, write to the address below.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change applies.
