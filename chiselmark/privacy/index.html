Chiselmark privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · Chiselmark for Android (package com.spokeshaveapps.chiselmark) · by Spokeshave Apps

In short: Chiselmark scans and makes QR codes and barcodes on your phone. What you scan, the codes you make, your history and any Wi-Fi passwords are never uploaded to us or anyone else. We have no servers and no accounts. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data under the ad privacy choices you make. Buying Pro removes ads.

1. Camera and images

Camera frames are read on your phone by Google’s ML Kit barcode model, which is built into the app, and are never saved or uploaded. Images you pick in the Android photo picker or share to Chiselmark are read the same way and are not copied or kept. Chiselmark never opens a link, joins a network or starts a payment by itself: you tap first. When it opens a link it removes common tracking tags (such as utm_source or fbclid) unless you turn that off; what the code says is kept unchanged in your history.

Two-factor (2FA) setup codes. If you scan an otpauth:// code, Chiselmark shows the account and passes the code to your authenticator app only when you tap. The secret key is never written to history or anywhere else, and screenshots are blocked while it is on screen.

Scanning for other apps. Other apps may ask Chiselmark to scan one code for them (the standard “Barcode Scanner” intent). The scanned text goes back to that app only, and is not added to your history.

3. What stays on your phone

In its private app storage, and only on your device, Chiselmark keeps: your settings, your scan history (unless you set history to Off), the codes you create, brand kits and logos you add, and on-device Insights counts (dates, code types and link domains, no contents). Exported images and PDFs are saved where you choose (Pictures or Download). History, codes and logos are excluded from Android cloud and device-transfer backups. Your Pro status is not stored; it is asked from Google Play. We cannot see any of it.

4. Ads (free version only)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Before any ad request, Chiselmark uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in some US states). You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

When ads are shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google: IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad and product interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID. Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Ads never receive what you scan or create.

Chiselmark Pro users see no ads. Once Google Play confirms your Pro purchase, the ads SDK and consent form are not started at all.

The ML Kit library may send Google anonymous diagnostics about its own performance; it never sends camera frames, images or decoded contents.

5. Purchases

Chiselmark Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app receives only whether you own Pro. See the Google Payments privacy notice.

6. Bug reports and feature requests

Settings → Report a bug / Request a feature prepares an email in your own email app. Nothing is sent until you tap Send there. It contains only what you type and, if you leave the switch on, the app version, Android version, phone model and app language. It never contains scans, codes or history. We use these emails only to fix bugs and plan features, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

7. Analytics

Chiselmark contains no analytics service. Insights are counted on your phone and shown only to you.

8. Children

Chiselmark is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them.

9. Deleting your data

Delete single scans or clear history in the app, or remove everything with Android Settings → Apps → Chiselmark → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Exported images and PDFs are normal files you can delete any time. To have a feedback email you sent us deleted, write to the address below.
